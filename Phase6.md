# Reverse AI Studio

# Phase 6 - Continuous Learning Platform

## Objective

Build an automated learning platform that continuously improves AI models using real business data from the Ticket Management System.

Instead of manually uploading videos for training, every completed reverse ticket becomes a potential AI training sample.

The platform should transform daily warehouse operations into a continuous AI learning pipeline.

---

# Vision

Every reviewed ticket should become knowledge.

Every reviewer decision should improve AI.

Every warehouse inspection video should contribute to the next generation of AI models.

The AI should continuously learn from human reviewers without requiring manual dataset creation.
